## Shake to Enable Torch — Privacy Policy

**The full, authoritative privacy policy is published here:**
**https://matteomark.github.io/**

This README is only a short summary. If anything here differs from the published
policy page, the published page prevails. Effective / last updated: 17 June 2026.

### In brief

- The app stores a small amount of data **only on your device** using Android
  `SharedPreferences` — shake counts, daily streak, achievements, and settings
  (sensitivity, theme). This on-device data is **never transmitted to the
  developer** (Luxebyte Labs operates no servers and no backend) and is never
  sold or shared. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.
- The app shows **one optional, opt-in rewarded video ad** — only when you tap
  to view your achievements and choose to watch it. It is never auto-played and
  appears nowhere else. To serve it, the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google
  AdMob)** collects and shares, for ad serving, measurement, and fraud
  prevention: the **Android Advertising ID (AAID)**, **app-set ID**, **IP
  address**, **device/diagnostic information**, and **ad interactions**. This
  data is collected by and shared with **Google and its advertising partners**
  and may be processed on servers **outside the EU/EEA, including the United
  States**. The developer receives none of it.
- In the **EEA, UK, and Switzerland**, a **Google UMP consent form** is shown
  **before any ad is requested**. You can change or withdraw consent anytime via
  the in-app **"Ad & privacy settings"**, and reset or delete your Advertising
  ID in Android settings.

See the published policy for purposes and legal bases (GDPR), third-party
recipients, international transfers, retention, your rights (GDPR and
CCPA/CPRA), and children's-privacy details.

### Permissions requested in the app

To work properly, Shake to Enable Torch requests the following permissions:

- **FLASHLIGHT, CAMERA** — to enable and disable the flashlight on your device.
- **VIBRATE** — to vibrate the device when the flashlight status changes.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC** — to run the service that
  listens for device motion so the app can work while in the background or
  closed. If you disable the service, the app will not run in the background.
- **REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS** — to let you open settings and
  disable battery optimization. This is optional and may improve reliability.
- **WAKE_LOCK** — to let the app work while the device is locked, so you can turn
  the flashlight on from the lock screen.
- **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED** — to automatically start the app when you turn on
  your device (only if you enable this).
- **READ_PHONE_STATE** — to disable the service during active and incoming calls
  (only if you enable this).
